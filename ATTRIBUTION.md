## What was brought in and adapted

- `skills/debug/SKILL.md`: the classify-first step (product defect / test defect / environment / flake), the "question the architecture after 3 failed fixes" rule, and the rigorous evidence-before-claiming-done framing.
- `skills/kaizen/SKILL.md`: the 3Ms of waste (Muda/Mura/Muri), the entropy-reduction questions, and the artifact-management (progressive disclosure) guidance.
- `skills/test/SKILL.md`: the Test-Driven Development section — the Iron Law, RED-GREEN-REFACTOR cycle, and common-rationalizations table.

None of these were copied verbatim: headings, examples, and surrounding prose were rewritten to match this pack's own house style and merged alongside pre-existing original content in the same files, but the techniques, rules, and rationale themselves originate from the source above.

## What was authored for this pack, not borrowed
