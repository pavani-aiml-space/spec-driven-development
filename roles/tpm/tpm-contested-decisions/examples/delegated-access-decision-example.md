# Worked example: a row that sits inside an existing RACI

One illustrative row on a Contested Decisions table, not a template to copy verbatim - the point is how its Approver reconciles against an existing RACI's Accountable owner, per Process step 4 and the Other notes section.

Continues the caregiver delegated-access program from `tpm-raci/examples/compliance-initiative-example.md`. That RACI names the **Compliance Lead** as Accountable (joint-Approver with Legal) on the "Privacy/regulatory sign-off" row. Partway through design, a specific question under that row stalls: which consent model to use. It gets added as a row on this program's Contested Decisions table:

| Question | Driver | Approver | Contributors | Informed | Deadline | Status | Decision | Rationale | Options Considered | Reversibility | Date Decided |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Does a delegate need to re-confirm consent every 90 days, or is one-time consent valid until explicitly revoked? | Product Manager | Compliance Lead | Legal, Engineering Lead, Design Lead | Sponsor, Support Lead, Security Lead | 2026-08-08 | Decided | One-time consent, valid until explicitly revoked, with a mandatory annual reminder notification (not re-confirmation) | Regulatory counsel confirmed 90-day re-confirmation isn't required for this data category; forcing it would add friction with no compliance benefit. An annual reminder balances user awareness against re-consent fatigue. | 90-day re-confirmation; one-time consent with no reminder; one-time consent with annual reminder | Two-way door - policy can be revised later if compliance guidance changes | 2026-07-30 |

**Why the Approver is the Compliance Lead, not the Product Manager:** this question is a sub-decision of the RACI's "Privacy/regulatory sign-off" row, where Compliance already holds Accountable (joint-Approver) authority. Naming the Product Manager as Approver here - even though they're driving the process and most invested in shipping fast - would be the authority collision the Other notes section warns about: a second, unreconciled owner for a decision the RACI already assigned. The Product Manager runs the process as Driver; Compliance still has final say.

Once decided, the follow-on work (updating the consent UX, writing the reminder-notification copy) goes back into the RACI's "Invite/accept UX flow" and "Access scope & consent model" rows - not tracked on this table. And since this row is now Decided with full context, no separate `decision-log` entry gets created for it - the log row (if this started there) just links to this page.
